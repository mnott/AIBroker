---
name: handover
description: Write your handover now, for your post-compact self. Use when the operator says /handover or asks for a handover.
---

Run `aibroker handover` in a shell. It types the same handover instruction the
AIBroker watcher sends before a compaction into this session. When it arrives,
do what it says: update the TODO.md Continue section and the session note, list
open workers and specs, record decisions and lessons, and commit. It works with
or without a goal; it never changes a goal.

To hand over another session: `aibroker handover <session>`.
